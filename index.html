Frequently asked questions

Can this be adapted to my agency?

Potentially. Send me your current process, the tools you use and an example of the output you need. I’ll confirm the scope and feasibility before quoting.

Will the workflow send emails automatically?

The featured offer focuses on research and draft preparation with human review. Any sending integration would need a separate scope and discussion.

Are the contact details guaranteed to be accurate?

No. Extracted contact information needs checking. An email found on a website is not the same as a verified, deliverable address.

How much does a project cost?

I quote after reviewing the task. A small pilot has an agreed scope and price before work begins. API usage, hosting and other software costs are discussed separately.

Is this a client case study?

No. The featured demo is a portfolio project showing the workflow and its outputs.
